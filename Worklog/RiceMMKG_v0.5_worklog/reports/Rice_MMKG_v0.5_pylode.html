riceMMKG

Description

  • Rice MMKG is a knowledge graph for rice disease and pest diagnosis, populated from the Paddy Doctor image dataset. It links diseases, pests, pathogens, symptoms, environmental factors, growth stages, and management knowledge, and separates raw dataset annotation (annotatedAs) from domain-knowledge evidence (captures/indicates). This is a minimal schema: every class holds individuals and every asserted property holds assertions -- nothing is declared ahead of the data that would populate it. See dcterms:description's sibling note on the deferred design roadmap for what was intentionally left out and why.

  • RiceMMKG: A Multimodal Knowledge Graph and Domain Ontology for Rice Pest and Disease Identification. Every domain-level assertion is grounded in peer-reviewed agronomic literature and authoritative databases (IRRI Rice Doctor Knowledge Bank, CABI Crop Protection Compendium, EPPO Global Database, and BBPOPT), formally traceable via OWL axiom annotations (owl:Axiom with dcterms:source and dcterms:bibliographicCitation).

IRI http://www.semanticweb.org/arifu/ontologies/2026/3/riceMMKG#annotatedAs
Description

  • Records the raw source-dataset label assigned to an Observation (e.g. a Paddy Doctor image), independent of the detects/detectedBy relation used for confirmed field or literature-backed detections.

  • The range is a union of Disease, HealthStatus, Pest, and Symptom because this property carries ground-truth annotations from the Paddy Doctor dataset, whose label set mixes diseases, pests, symptoms, and a healthy class in a single flat vocabulary. It records what the dataset asserts, not a classification conclusion drawn by this ontology.

detects op

IRI http://www.semanticweb.org/arifu/ontologies/2026/3/riceMMKG#detects
Description

Range deliberately excludes Disease: a disease is a condition and is never directly observed -- no modality perceives a fungus in a field. What can be observed directly is the organism (a pest by eye, a pathogen by lab confirmation). A claim like "this image detects blast" is a conclusion and belongs on the annotation path (annotatedAs/denotes), not here.
