# Competency Questions for a Rice Pest & Disease Multimodal Knowledge Graph

## Purpose and scope

This multimodal knowledge graph (MMKG) is intended to support early diagnosis, evidence integration, and decision-making for major rice pests and diseases by semantically linking textual knowledge, field imagery, agroclimatic sensor observations, and genomic/tabular records. The target use cases include research, plant-pathology and extension decision support, and precision-agriculture systems operating primarily in tropical lowland rice environments, with emphasis on Indonesia and Southeast Asia while retaining a schema suitable for international reuse.

### Assumptions used to execute the prompt

Because the project-specific input fields were left unspecified, this specification uses the prompt's defaults: common major rice pests and diseases are in scope; text, image, sensor, and genomic/tabular sources are assumed to be available in generic form; the target is 40 CQs; no pre-existing project ontology or CQ set is assumed; and alignment should be considered with resources such as AGROVOC, Crop Ontology, Plant Trait Ontology, Plant Phenotype Ontology, and relevant disease-ontology design patterns. Named examples include rice blast (*Magnaporthe oryzae*), bacterial leaf blight (*Xanthomonas oryzae* pv. *oryzae*), sheath blight (*Rhizoctonia solani*), rice tungro, brown planthopper (*Nilaparvata lugens*), stem borer damage, and rice bug damage. Where a CQ refers to a risk score, diagnostic confidence, threshold, or severity class, the KG is assumed either to store that derived value explicitly or to represent the rule needed to compute it reproducibly.

## Scope boundary note

In scope are rice pest/disease entities, causal agents and vectors, symptoms and image phenotypes, environmental observations, rice varieties and resistance/susceptibility information, agronomic/yield outcomes, management recommendations, provenance, and the semantic links required to combine these evidence types for diagnosis and decision support. Out of scope are general rice-production economics, unrelated crop species, autonomous pesticide application, unrestricted causal claims not represented by evidence, and raw machine-learning model design except where model outputs or explainable derived features are stored as KG entities. CQs CQ-ENV-04, CQ-ENV-05, CQ-MM-05, CQ-MM-06, CQ-MM-10 to CQ-MM-14, and CQ-MM-16 may be infeasible unless sufficiently dense, time-aligned, georeferenced, and quality-controlled sensor, image, diagnosis, genotype, and outcome data are available; CQ-GEN-04 and CQ-GEN-05 additionally require harmonized multi-environment trial and resistance annotations. These potentially infeasible CQs should be retained as aspirational requirements but explicitly tagged during implementation according to data availability and evidential confidence.
